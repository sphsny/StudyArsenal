About "Study Arsenal"!


This single page application was developed using HTML, CSS, jQuery and JSON
as part of the first year of my BSc (Hons) Computer Science degree.

Study Arsenal is designed to have everything in place to support your studies!

- Track your study time across different subjects
- Name study sessions and add notes
- Keep track of tasks in a to-do list
- Keep up with deadlines in a calendar

All of the collected data is stored in your browser using local storage.
As a user, you have full control of your data and can delete it at anytime.
However, keep in mind that once deleted, no data can be restored.

Happy studying!
